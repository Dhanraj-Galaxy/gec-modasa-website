About Department

Department of Computer Engineering

The Department of Computer Engineering at Government Engineering College, Modasa provides technical education in computer engineering, programming, software development, networking, databases and modern technologies.

The department emphasizes theoretical knowledge, practical skills, problem solving, innovation and research to prepare students for higher education and professional careers.

Government Engineering College, Modasa was established in 1984 under the Directorate of Technical Education, Gujarat State.

College Information

Established: 1984

University: Gujarat Technological University

Recognition: AICTE

Location: Modasa, Gujarat
